Trump Accounts: How to Get the $1,000 for Your Baby (Form 4547)

If your child was born in 2025, 2026, 2027 or 2028 and has a Social Security number, the U.S. Treasury will put a one-time $1,000 into a Trump Account for them, but only if you sign up. You can sign up with Form 4547 when you file your taxes, or online through your IRS account or trumpaccounts.gov.

By LaCaya Pope, IRS PTIN P03232493 · Updated November 2026

Trump Accounts in plain English

What is a Trump Account?

A new savings and investment account for children, set up under the 2025 tax law. It works a lot like a retirement account (IRA) for kids: the money is invested and grows, and it’s generally meant to stay in the account until your child turns 18.

Who gets the free $1,000?

Children born between January 1, 2025 and December 31, 2028 who are U.S. citizens with a Social Security number. The Treasury deposits a one-time $1,000 into the child’s account through a pilot program.

It is not automatic

A parent or other authorized adult has to make two choices: open the Trump Account, and elect the $1,000 pilot contribution. If you skip the election, your child doesn’t get the $1,000.

How to sign up

Option 1: file Form 4547 with your tax return. If your baby was born in 2026, this goes with the return you file in 2027, and I’ll include it when I prepare your return.
Option 2: sign up online through your IRS Individual Online Account or at trumpaccounts.gov.

Older kids can have one too

Any child under 18 with a Social Security number can have a Trump Account opened, but only babies born 2025 through 2028 get the $1,000 from the Treasury.

Can family add money?

Yes. Contributions started July 4, 2026. Parents, relatives and others can add up to $5,000 a year in total, and some employers can contribute too. Check trumpaccounts.gov for the current limits and how to deposit.

Before you file: have your child’s Social Security card and birth date ready. If you’re claiming a 2025–2028 baby on your return, ask your preparer about Form 4547 so you don’t leave the $1,000 behind. Also see the tax season 2027 key dates.
