How 1099 taxes work

When you're paid on a 1099, no one takes taxes out for you. You owe two federal taxes on your profit (what's left after business expenses):

  • Self-employment tax: Social Security and Medicare, 15.3% of 92.35% of your profit, about 14.1%. It applies once your net earnings from self-employment are $400 or more.
  • Federal income tax: figured on your total income from all sources, after the standard deduction, half of your self-employment tax and the qualified business income deduction.

2026 estimated tax due dates

  • April 15, 2026: income from January through March
  • June 15, 2026: April and May
  • September 15, 2026: June through August
  • January 15, 2027: September through December

Pay online with IRS Direct Pay (choose "Estimated Tax" and Form 1040-ES for tax year 2026) or through your IRS Online Account. Missed earlier dates? Pay what you can now; the penalty is figured on how late each amount is.

Questions people ask about 1099 taxes

How much should I set aside for taxes on 1099 income?

Many people with 1099 income set aside somewhere between 15% and 30% of their profit, but the right amount depends on your filing status, your other income and your expenses. The calculator above shows your own percentage. Self-employment tax alone is about 14.1% of your profit, before any income tax.

What is self-employment tax?

Self-employment tax is Social Security and Medicare tax for people who work for themselves. It is 15.3% of 92.35% of your net profit (about 14.1% of profit). You owe it once your net earnings from self-employment are $400 or more, even if you owe no income tax.

Do I have to pay taxes on 1099 income if I didn't get a 1099?

Yes. All business income is taxable whether or not you receive a Form 1099. Starting with payments made in 2026, businesses generally send a 1099-NEC only when they pay you $2,000 or more, so keep your own income records.

When are 1099 estimated tax payments due?

For tax year 2026 the four estimated payment dates are April 15, 2026, June 15, 2026, September 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027. You can pay online with IRS Direct Pay or through your IRS Online Account.

What happens if I don't pay quarterly taxes?

You may owe an underpayment penalty on top of your tax. You can generally avoid it if your withholding and estimated payments cover at least 90% of this year's tax or 100% of last year's tax (110% if last year's adjusted gross income was over $150,000).

Can business expenses lower my 1099 taxes?

Yes. Ordinary and necessary business expenses lower your profit, which lowers both self-employment tax and income tax. Keep receipts and records for every expense you claim.
