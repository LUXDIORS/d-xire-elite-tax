1099 & Self-Employed Questions

What expenses can I legally write off as a 1099 independent contractor?

A self-employed professional may be able to deduct ordinary and necessary business expenses, depending on the work and the records kept. Examples can include qualifying business mileage, a qualifying home office, software, tools, and equipment. Some expenses must be allocated between business and personal use, and health-insurance deductions have additional eligibility rules. Keep receipts and records, and review your situation before claiming an expense.

Can you help me file taxes if I have both W-2 and 1099 income?

Yes. We prepare eligible individual returns that include W-2 wages and 1099 or other self-employment income. We review each income source and its records, then consider credits and deductions that may apply to your circumstances. Learn more about our mixed-income returns.

More Questions

Why might you ask follow-up questions?

Some tax rules require us to verify details and document eligibility for credits or filing positions. We take those due diligence checks seriously.

Can preparation guarantee no audit or balance due?

We’re committed to giving every return a careful, thorough review. That helps us catch errors, spot potential tax due before filing, and reduce avoidable amendments and preventable issues as much as possible. We take time to ask questions and explain what we find, so you have a clearer picture before your return is filed. No preparer can guarantee that the IRS won’t select a return for examination or that no tax will be owed, but careful preparation helps us minimize surprises and support you along the way.
