Virtual Tax Preparation for W-2 Households & Mixed Income Filers

Clear guidance through the details. I’m here to help you understand your household’s full income picture and the tax information that applies to you.

W-2, multiple jobs, and 1099 income

Tax preparation that considers your whole situation.

Filing can feel complicated when you balance multiple jobs, combine W-2 wages with 1099 work, start a side gig, or manage household details. I’ll help you sort through the documents and explain what matters for your individual return.

At D-XIRE Elite Tax, I review the income and records you provide, look at the circumstances that may affect your filing, and work to identify credits and deductions you may qualify for under the applicable rules. Your tax outcome depends on your facts; no particular refund is guaranteed.

01 · Household

W-2 household credits and dependents

I review the dependent information you provide and consider whether credits such as the Child Tax Credit, Earned Income Tax Credit, or Child and Dependent Care Credit may apply. Each credit has its own eligibility rules and documentation requirements.

02 · Multiple income sources

W-2 wages plus 1099 income

I bring your wage and contractor income together on your individual return and review the records you provide for accurate reporting. If you also have side work, see examples of self-employed and 1099 work we serve.

03 · Education

Education and student loan information

I can review qualified tuition records and student loan interest information to see whether the American Opportunity Tax Credit, Lifetime Learning Credit, or student loan interest deduction may apply. Eligibility depends on the tax rules and your circumstances.

04 · Deductions

Standard or itemized deduction

I’ll compare the standard deduction with eligible itemized deductions based on your records. Depending on the rules and your circumstances, that review may include charitable gifts, eligible medical expenses, and state and local taxes.

I provide one-to-one virtual support for eligible individual returns in all 50 states. I’ll listen, explain what paperwork is needed and why, and carefully review the information you share as I prepare your return.
