D-XIRE Referral Partner Program Payout Tiers

Approved partners can earn cash rewards when a new client completes eligible tax preparation and either pays or receives a refund. Rewards follow non-retroactive tiers based on total successful referrals.

The amount is based on each client’s position in your qualifying referral count. The tiers are non-retroactive: each client earns the rate for the tier that client reaches; earlier referrals keep their earlier rate.

  • 1–5 Referrals:$50per client
  • 6–10 Referrals:$100per client
  • 11–15 Referrals:$150per client
  • 16–19 Referrals:$200per client
  • 20+ Referrals:$300per client

Rewards apply only to qualifying new clients who complete eligible tax preparation and either pay or receive a refund. Waitlist signups, inquiries, estimates, cancelled returns, duplicate referrals, existing clients, and clients who have neither paid nor received a refund do not qualify.
