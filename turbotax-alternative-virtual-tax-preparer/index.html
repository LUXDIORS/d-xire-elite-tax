TurboTax vs. a Virtual Tax Preparer: Find the Right Fit for Freelancers & Households

Choosing between filing on your own and working with a tax professional depends on the kind of help you want, the details in your return, and your budget. Here’s a straightforward look at TurboTax’s options and D-XIRE Elite Tax.

At a glance

Different levels of support for different needs.

TurboTax offers both do-it-yourself products and an expert full-service option. D-XIRE offers one-to-one preparation for eligible individual returns, including W-2, 1099, and mixed-income situations.

A practical choice

It’s okay to want someone in your corner.

DIY software can work well for people who prefer to prepare their own return. If your return includes contract work, side income, family changes, or questions you want to talk through, personal support may be a better fit for you.

Keep it self-directed

Choose DIY software if…

You’re comfortable gathering your records, answering the software prompts, and reviewing the result before filing.

Hand off preparation

Consider full service if…

You want a tax expert through a software provider to prepare your return under that service’s current terms.

Work one-to-one

Talk with D-XIRE if…

You want to work directly with LaCaya on an eligible individual return, including W-2, mixed-income, or self-employment details.
